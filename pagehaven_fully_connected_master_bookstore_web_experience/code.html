Frequently Asked Questions

What is the 14-day doorstep return policy? expand_more

Any physical book or magazine can be returned within 14 days of delivery. We arrange a doorstep courier pickup at zero charge. Books should remain unmarked with dust jackets intact.

How do instant e-book and digital folios work? expand_more

Purchased e-books and periodicals sync instantly to your cloud sanctuary library. You can also download DRM-free EPUB/MOBI files to your Kindle, Kobo, or tablet.

What if my book arrives with bumped corners or misprints? expand_more

Tap "Damaged Book" above and upload a snapshot. Our Care sanctuary will dispatch a brand-new replacement immediately without waiting for the damaged return.
