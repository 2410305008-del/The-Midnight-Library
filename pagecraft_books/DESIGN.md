## Brand & Style

The design system merges the sensory warmth of an archival bookstore with the sharp precision of a state-of-the-art digital reading environment. The emotional experience balances intellectual tranquility and contemporary utility—reminiscent of leafing through high-grade deckle-edge paper under focused task lighting. 

The aesthetic is **Warm Literary Editorial meets Modern Digital Reader**:
- **Tactility without Clutter:** Ivory and warm-paper surfaces ground the interface in physical bookmaking tradition, avoiding the cold clinical feel of standard e-commerce.
- **Deep Ink Typographic Dominance:** Rich obsidian ink provides definitive structure, mimicking hot-metal letterpress clarity.
- **Modern Digital Precision:** Structural elements leverage razor-thin framing, disciplined spacing rhythms, and luminous micro-accents to signal fast digital access, immediate cloud downloads, and contextual AI reader features.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate physical atmosphere while maintaining high legibility and precise product differentiation:

- **Surface & Backgrounds:** 
  - Canvas Base: `#FBF8F3` (Warm Book Paper)
  - Surface Raised / Cards: `#FFFFFF` (Brightened Vellum)
  - Surface Subdued: `#F3EFE6` (Antique Paper tint for secondary groupings and search rails)
- **Ink & Neutral Hierarchy:**
  - Ink Primary: `#121826` (Deep Obsidian Ink, 100% contrast for body and display)
  - Ink Subdued: `#4B5565` (Charcoal Muted for secondary metadata, ISBNs, and specs)
  - Border Neutral: `#E6DFD5` (Soft rule lines echoing deckled edges)
- **Interactive Accents:**
  - Primary Action / Accent: `#D97706` (Amber/Burnished Gold for primary purchase workflows, reading progress, and star ratings)
  - Tertiary Accent: `#E05A47` (Terracotta Ink for special sales, limited edition flags, and instant checkout)
- **Semantic Format Markers:**
  - Hardcopy Badge: `#4A6B6C` (Muted Sage/Slate, symbolizing cloth-bound hardcovers and archival permanence)
  - E-Book Badge: `#0F766E` (Deep Teal Cyan, representing immediate digital delivery)
  - AI Assistant Glow: `#F59E0B` with subtle `#8B5CF6` atmospheric dispersion (A clean dual-hue luminous highlight denoting smart literary summaries and contextual analysis)

## Typography

The type system brings classic print aesthetics together with functional screen legibility:

- **Editorial Headings (`Newsreader`):** Selected for its optical sizing and warm, humanist serif construction. It carries the weight and dignity of traditional book typesetting without looking historicist. Italics should be used selectively for book titles, pull quotes, and author accolades.
- **Interface & Metadata Body (`Manrope`):** A high-performance geometric sans-serif that remains crisp at dense scales (catalog filters, inventory counts, pricing tables, reader customization panels). 
- **Type Hierarchy Rules:** Headings larger than `headline-md` use optical kerning adjustments to mimic physical typesetting. In dense catalog grids, book titles prioritize `title-editorial` to preserve the visual weight of individual volumes, while transactional numbers, formats, and badges always resolve in `Manrope`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a responsive 12-column grid system calibrated to mimic the balanced proportions of open book spreads:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `margin: 2.5rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Editorial showcases and two-up reader previews can break out of container margins into asymmetric column spans (e.g., 5-column book spotlight with a 7-column excerpt).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Catalog listings switch to 2- or 3-column structures with sticky filter headers.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4 columns with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Book collections collapse into smooth horizontally scrolling shelves or single-column stacked review layouts.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Component interiors rely on an 8px base rhythm (`space-sm` for compact badge tags, `space-md` for standard card padding, `space-xl` for breathability between catalog chapters and curations).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, atmospheric layering over artificial elevation:

- **Tonal Substrates:** Rather than heavy drop shadows, base depth is achieved via surface tonal shifts: `#FBF8F3` (canvas) to `#FFFFFF` (card plane) to `#F3EFE6` (embedded panel).
- **Ambient Page Cast (Shadows):** When cards lift on hover or modals surface, shadows take on an amber-ink hue: `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(18, 24, 38, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.04)`.
- **Low-Contrast Framing:** All panels and cards utilize a hairline outline (`1px solid #E6DFD5`), ensuring crisp structural separation against the warm canvas.
- **Luminescent Overlays:** Floating cart counts and AI Reader triggers leverage a soft 12px blur radius with a low-opacity amber halo (`0 0 16px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.28)`), evoking a focused reading lamp in a dim study.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard. Containers, book mockups, and structural modules carry subtle `4px` (`0.25rem`) corners, maintaining the crisp lines of cut paper and bound cloth covers.

- **Structural Elements (Cards, Panels, Inputs):** Set to `0.25rem` (`rounded`) or `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) to preserve formal editorial geometry.
- **Interactive Badges & Status Pills:** Intentionally break the standard grid with full pill curves (`9999px`) to create distinction between content modules (square/structured) and contextual actions/tags (fluid/pill).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Purchase / Checkout):** Deep obsidian background (`#121826`), warm paper text (`#FBF8F3`), `0.25rem` radius. On hover: shifts smoothly to `#D97706` with subtle amber bloom.
- **Secondary (Add to Library / Preview):** 1px border (`#121826`), transparent background, ink text. Hover fills to `#F3EFE6`.
- **Ghost / Reading Action:** Neutral text with a subtle underline that expands outward from center on hover.

### Format Chips & Badges
- **Hardcopy Badge:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), background `#4A6B6C` at 12% opacity, text `#324B4C`, typography `label-sm`. Accompanied by a subtle debossed cloth-style icon.
- **E-Book Instant Badge:** Pill-shaped, background `#0F766E` at 12% opacity, text `#0F766E`, typography `label-sm`. Accompanied by a fast-download lightning bolt or digital screen glyph.
- **Floating Cart Badge:** Pinned to top navigation or bottom-right floating rail. Obsidian circle with amber numeric indicator (`#D97706`) and slight ambient drop shadow.

### Cards (Book Grid & Reader Previews)
- **Structure:** Crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #E6DFD5` border, `space-md` internal padding, `rounded` corners.
- **Cover Display:** 3:4 aspect ratio container with an ultra-subtle spine gradient overlay on the left margin (giving flat cover artwork a realistic book-binding depth).
- **Format Selector:** Embedded segmented control at the bottom of the card allowing users to toggle between Hardcopy and E-book pricing instantly before checkout.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Search Bar:** Elongated pill or soft-corner input with `#F3EFE6` fill, no border in default state, transitioning to `1px solid #D97706` on focus. Placeholder text in muted charcoal serif (`Newsreader` italic) to mimic conversational literary inquiry ("Search by author, theme, or quote...").

### Form Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** Custom square with `2px` border radius, `#E6DFD5` border, filling with `#121826` and an off-white checkmark on selection.
- **Radio Buttons (Edition Selectors):** Concentric ink circle filled with an amber center pip (`#D97706`).

### Specialized Component: AI Literary Companion
- **Trigger:** A glowing pill chip featuring an amber-violet gradient border (`#D97706` to `#8B5CF6`) and a soft breathing ambient backlight.
- **Drawer / Popover:** Parchment card surface (`#FDFBF7`) showing instant chapter digests, thematic analysis, and reading difficulty ratings with subtle monospaced metadata highlights.
