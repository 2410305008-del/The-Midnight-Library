## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an evocative, tranquil literary sanctuary. It unites the thoughtful, contemplative heritage of classical publishing with a modern, breathable digital storefront. The aesthetic evokes the atmosphere of an airy, sunlit private library overlooking cool waters: crisp vellum, tailored bindings, cool porcelain surfaces, and ink-toned typography.

### Core Character
- **Atmospheric Clarity:** Airy, luminous compositions prioritizing quiet discovery over visual clamor.
- **Literary Poise:** Authoritative, high-contrast serif headlines paired with precise, functional sans-serif UI elements.
- **Architectural Calm:** Generous margins, disciplined horizontal baselines, and structured editorial framing that echo traditional typesetting.
- **Tactile Subtlety:** Micro-interactions and borders emulate deckle-edged paper, blind debossing, and fine fountain-pen strokes rather than digital gloss.

Target audience includes discerning readers, collectors, independent publishers, and intellectual explorers seeking respite from noisy, algorithmic marketplaces.

## Colors

The palette balances cool, contemplative slate-cerulean with translucent icy whites and deep archival navies.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1e6091` - Scholarly Cerulean):** Grounds main interaction points, focal headlines, primary actions, and selected states.
- **Secondary (`#38bdf8` - Sky Mist):** Used sparingly for focal points, live status indicators, bookmark ribbons, and delicate micro-highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#0284c7` - Deep Coastal Azure):** Drives interactive hover states, active links, and secondary metric indicators.
- **Neutral & Ink (`#0f172a` / `#1e293b`):** Deep carbon and archival navy for editorial copy, ensuring peerless contrast without the harshness of pure black.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#f8fafc` (Cool Porcelain) creating a serene, glare-free backdrop.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards, Folios):** `#ffffff` (Pure Vellum) bounded by faint ice-blue outlines.
- **Surface Tint (Accents & Highlights):** `#f0f7ff` (Ice Blue Tint) for nested quotes, collection trays, and input containers.
- **Subtle Surface (Badges & Inactive Elements):** `#e0f2fe` (Pale Glaze) paired with deep slate text.
- **Border Tone:** `#cbd5e1` transitioning to `#bae6fd` on hover or focused states.

## Typography

The typographical strategy employs an intentional tension between editorial grace and technical legibility.

- **Headlines & Editorial Quotes (`Newsreader`):** Selected for its bookish, literary rhythm and expressive italic forms. It conveys narrative depth across book titles, section headers, author names, and literary pull quotes.
- **Interface & Mechanics (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides clean geometric structure, wide apertures, and exceptional clarity for search fields, faceted filtering, pricing, navigation labels, and metadata chips.
- **Editorial Hierarchy:** Display headlines must honor optical breathing room; always couple large serif display titles with ample vertical clearance and muted sans-serif category eyebrow markers set in uppercase with slight tracking (`label-sm`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a 12-column responsive fluid grid with fixed maximum content containment at `1280px` to maintain optimal book jacket proportions and reading line lengths.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Grids
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, `2.5rem` (`40px`) page margins. Maximum line width for reading content is constrained to `65ch`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters, `2rem` (`32px`) margins. Sidebar filters collapse into an ambient slide-over drawer.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, `1rem` (`16px`) margins. Catalog items transition to 2-column book showcases or single-column editorial cards.

### Spacing Rhythm
Component spacing follows an 8pt modular rhythm:
- Micro-spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) governs icon-to-label offsets and card tag clusters.
- Component-interior spacing (`space-md`, `space-lg`) maintains light breathing room within cards, drawers, and form modules.
- Macro-spacing (`space-xl` and higher) partitions distinct catalog curatorships and editorial long-form essays.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows and aggressive elevation planes in favor of luminous, layered surface tints and delicate translucent borders.

### Surface Stratification
- **Ground Floor:** `#f8fafc` matte porcelain.
- **Level 1 (Default Cards & Shelves):** `#ffffff` solid background bounded by a `1px` subtle outline of `rgba(186, 230, 253, 0.45)` (Sky Glaze).
- **Level 2 (Active Sheets, Floating Menus & Hovered Volumes):** `#ffffff` elevated by a whisper-light tinted shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 96, 145, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Reader Panels):** Layered over a soft frosted backdrop (`rgba(240, 247, 255, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), bounded by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`.

Transitions between elevation tiers should favor calm ease-out curves (300ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)) simulating the gentle lifting of fine stationery.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined, subtly softened architectural border radius (`roundedness: 1`).

- **Base Components (0.25rem / 4px):** Standard inputs, dropdown lists, book jacket thumbnails, checkboxes, and inline badges. This preserves the crisp, rectangular lines of physical books and fine letterpress cards.
- **Cards & Containers (0.5rem / 8px):** Product cards, editorial modules, and modular layout panels.
- **Overlays & Dialogs (0.75rem / 12px):** Modal sheets, popovers, and sticky navigational banners.
- **Exceptions:** Search pills and bookmark tag chips can adopt full circular endpoints (`9999px`) strictly when functioning as micro-filters or solitary status tokens.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#1e6091` with `#ffffff` text, `4px` radius, subtle inner border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`). On hover: shifts to `#0284c7` with a gentle `0 4px 12px rgba(30, 96, 145, 0.2)` glow.
- **Secondary / Outline:** `#ffffff` surface, `1px solid #bae6fd` border, `#1e6091` text. On hover: `#f0f7ff` background and `#0284c7` border.
- **Ghost:** Transparent canvas, `#1e293b` text; hovers reveal `#f0f7ff` with deep slate blue text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curated Category Tags:** `#f0f7ff` container, `#1e6091` text, `1px solid #e0f2fe` border.
- **Edition Indicator (Hardcover, First Edition):** `#ffffff` background with fine `#cbd5e1` outline and `Newsreader` italic detail text.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Standard Search:** High-luminosity `#ffffff` background, `1px solid #cbd5e1` border, inset soft inner reflection.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#1e6091` accompanied by a `3px` focus ring of `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`. Includes an ink-navy placeholder (`#64748b`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Form Controls:** Fine `1.5px` border in `#94a3b8` over `#ffffff`.
- **Selected State:** `#1e6091` fill with crisp white glyph. Radios feature a clean inset porcelain dot.

### Book & Editorial Cards
- **Product Tiles:** `#ffffff` vellum surface, `4px` border radius, encased in a low-contrast `1px solid #e2e8f0` stroke. Hover elevates the card slightly (`-2px` Y-axis) and enriches the border to `#bae6fd`.
- **Book Cover Display:** Displayed with an aspect ratio of 2:3, framed by a delicate inset border (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`) that simulates page-depth spine shading.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Reading Progress Spine:** A slender horizontal bar featuring a `#e0f2fe` track and a `#38bdf8` progression fill.
- **Curator’s Excerpt Ribbon:** A porcelain (`#f0f7ff`) blockquote callout with a vertical `#1e6091` left accent border (3px width) pairing `Newsreader` italic typography with a quiet editorial attribution footer.
